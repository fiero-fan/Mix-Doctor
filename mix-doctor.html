    Stereo

    One-time setup

    1. Install BlackHole 2ch (free): existential.audio/blackhole
    2. Open Audio MIDI Setup (in Applications › Utilities) → + → Create Multi-Output Device. Tick your headphones/interface first, then BlackHole 2ch, and enable Drift Correction on BlackHole.
    3. In Logic: Settings › Audio › Output Device → Multi-Output Device. You still hear everything; BlackHole gets a copy.
    4. Open this page as a file in Chrome on your Mac (double-click the downloaded mix-doctor.html, or drag it into Chrome). The hosted claude.ai version can't open audio inputs.
    5. Click Start listening, allow access, choose BlackHole 2ch, and press play in Logic.
